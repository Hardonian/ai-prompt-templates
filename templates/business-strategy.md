# Business & Strategy Prompt Templates

## Market Research
```
Analyze the [INDUSTRY] market for [PRODUCT/SERVICE]. Include:
1. Market size and growth rate (last 3 years)
2. Top 5 competitors with strengths/weaknesses
3. Target customer demographics
4. Key trends shaping the market
5. Barriers to entry
6. Opportunities for differentiation
7. Regulatory considerations

Format as a structured report with bullet points.
```

**Example:** Analyze the project management software market for a team collaboration tool aimed at remote startups...

**Tips:** Be specific about geography and segment. "Global enterprise market" gives different results than "US SMB market."

## Competitor Analysis
```
Compare [YOUR PRODUCT] against these competitors: [COMPETITOR 1], [COMPETITOR 2], [COMPETITOR 3].

Create a comparison table covering:
- Pricing model
- Key features (top 10)
- Target audience
- Pros and cons
- Market positioning
- Customer reviews sentiment
- Unique selling propositions

Then identify 3 underserved niches where [YOUR PRODUCT] could win.
```

## Business Plan Generator
```
Create a lean business plan for [BUSINESS IDEA] targeting [AUDIENCE].

Include:
1. Problem statement (1 paragraph)
2. Solution description (1 paragraph)
3. Unique value proposition (1 sentence)
4. Revenue model (list pricing tiers)
5. Cost structure (fixed vs variable)
6. Key metrics to track (first 12 months)
7. Go-to-market strategy (3 channels)
8. Risk assessment (top 3 risks + mitigation)

Keep it concise — max 2 pages if written out.
```

## Financial Analysis
```
Analyze these financial metrics for [COMPANY/PRODUCT]:
- Revenue: [AMOUNT]
- Costs: [AMOUNT]
- Growth rate: [PERCENTAGE]
- Customer count: [NUMBER]
- Churn rate: [PERCENTAGE]

Calculate:
1. Gross margin
2. Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
3. Customer lifetime value (LTV)
4. LTV:CAC ratio
5. Monthly burn rate
6. Runway (months)

Provide recommendations for improving unit economics.
```

## Customer Persona Builder
```
Create a detailed customer persona for [PRODUCT/SERVICE].

Include:
- Name and demographics (age, location, income, job title)
- Goals and motivations (top 3)
- Pain points (top 5)
- Technology comfort level
- Buying process (how they discover, evaluate, decide)
- Objections they'd raise
- Preferred communication channels
- A day-in-the-life scenario
- Quote that captures their mindset

Make it specific and actionable for marketing.
```

## Sales Email Sequence
```
Write a 5-email cold outreach sequence for [PRODUCT/SERVICE] targeting [AUDIENCE].

Email 1: Hook + value prop (under 100 words)
Email 2: Social proof + case study (under 120 words)
Email 3: Pain point agitation (under 100 words)
Email 4: Offer + urgency (under 80 words)
Email 5: Breakup email (under 60 words)

Include subject lines for each. Tone: [professional/casual/urgent].
```

## Proposal Writer
```
Write a project proposal for [CLIENT] for [PROJECT DESCRIPTION].

Include:
1. Executive summary (3 sentences)
2. Problem/opportunity statement
3. Proposed solution (detailed)
4. Timeline with milestones
5. Deliverables list
6. Pricing (breakdown by phase)
7. Terms and conditions
8. Why us (differentiators)

Tone: [professional/technical/friendly]. Length: [1-3 pages].
```
